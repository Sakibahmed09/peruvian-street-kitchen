# PSK Website

## Design Context

### Users
East London halal food crowd, 16–35, group-chat driven, mobile-first (Instagram bio traffic). HMC-conscious families ordering Friday-night boxes. They know Nando's, Assenheims, Shah's Halal, German Doner Kebab.

### Brand Personality
Loud, hand-painted, hungry. Street-sign confidence, not corporate polish. Victorian playbill meets Lima street cart meets East London chicken shop pride. The printed black paper bag IS the brand; the website is the bag brought to life.

### Aesthetic Direction
Street playbill maximalism. Ink black (#131312) base, stone paper (#E2D5C5) sections with torn edges, leaf green (#68954F) and flame red (#E33B25) accents only, crimson (#920D09) and forest (#1B3C1D) for playbill type on stone. Print grain over everything. Hard offset shadows, rotated stamps and price tags, tape and stickers, marquee ticker strips. Type: Big Shoulders Display (poster caps) + Alfa Slab One (victorian slab moments) + Libre Franklin (body). Anti-references: generic AI dark-mode SaaS, glassmorphism, gradient text, yellow accents from the old site.

### Design Principles
1. The bag is the art direction: every surface should feel printed, stamped or taped, never floated or glassy.
2. The green sauce is the hero product (Shah's white-sauce playbook); "Peru not Peri" is the fight we pick (Nando's).
3. Real quotes with real names, set like a playbill, are the proof layer. Never invent testimonials.
4. Food photography lives on ink-black slate with moody side light and steam; generated shots must stay true to the real dishes.
5. Every section earns a background: ink, stone, crimson or flame. Alternate for pacing; separate with torn paper edges.
