How PSK started

The chicken I could only get at work

Two brothers who couldn’t leave a sauce alone.

I worked in an office in central London, and downstairs there was a Peruvian chicken spot I got a bit obsessed with. Grilled thigh, that green sauce, the lot. The catch was I could only ever get it there. Leave the office and it was gone. Nothing near home came close.

Around the same time my brother got back from New York still going on about the white sauce off the halal carts. He’d brought the recipe home with him.

So we started messing about in the kitchen. Me chasing the green sauce I missed from work, him building on the cart white sauce from New York. Cooking, tasting, arguing, cooking again.

We’re not chefs. We just couldn’t stop until the box tasted like the one worth queuing for.

The early versions were nowhere near it. But we’d done this before with Simply Smashed, so we knew the recipe is maybe a fifth of the work. The rest is doing it again and again until it’s right every single time.

Then the Peruvian Thigh sold out in three hours on a Monday. That’s when we knew we had something.

— Sakib, PSK

Where the sauces come from

No culinary school. No textbook. One green sauce chased from a Peruvian spot under a London office. One white sauce carried back from the halal carts of New York. Months of cooking between two brothers until both were right.
